Support

Questions, bugs or feedback? Email stoiaapp@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. An account is only needed for Circles and the Stoic Mentor.

Where is my journal stored? Only on your device. See the privacy policy.

How do I cancel Premium? In iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Stoia. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I restore a purchase? Open Account from the person icon on the Today screen and tap Restore purchases.

How do I delete my account? In the app, open Account from the person icon on the Today screen and tap Delete account. Your account and everything you shared in circles are deleted right away; your journal stays on your device. If you can't open the app, email stoiaapp@gmail.com from the address you signed in with.
