Stoia

Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

Stoia is a Stoic philosophy app for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, made by Neka Tsignadze ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information Stoia handles, why, and the choices you have. The short version: your journal stays on your device. We only receive what you choose to share, and the free plan shows ads from Google.

1. Information that stays on your device

Everything you write or record in your daily practice is stored only on your device (and on your Apple Watch and widgets through your device's shared app storage). This includes your name and birthdate if you enter them, your goals, morning and evening reflections, virtue scores, journal and commonplace entries, challenge notes and photos, highlights, reading progress and reminder times. We do not receive it and cannot read it. Deleting the app deletes this information, unless your device backs it up to your own iCloud or computer backup.

Apple Health. If you allow it, Stoia reads your workouts and mindful minutes to credit discomfort challenges, and saves the time you spend in Stoia's breathing and reflection exercises as mindful minutes. Health data never leaves your device through Stoia, is never sent to us or to advertisers, and is never used for advertising or marketing.

Notifications are scheduled on your device; no notification content is sent to us.

2. Optional account

You can use Stoia without an account. An account is needed only for Circles and the Stoic Mentor. If you sign in with Apple or with an email sign-in link or code, we store:

  • your account identifier and the email address used to sign in (with Sign in with Apple, this may be a private relay address from Apple);
  • the display name you choose, which members of your circles can see.

3. Circles

Circles are small private groups you join by invite code. When you choose to share something into a circle, such as an evening review, a challenge or a lesson, that entry is stored on our servers and shown to the members of that circle, together with reactions. Nothing from your journal is shared unless you share it. You can delete your shared entries and leave a circle at any time.

4. The Stoic Mentor

When you ask the Mentor a question (Premium), the situation you describe and relevant public-domain passages are sent through our server to Anthropic, which provides the AI model that writes the reply. We do not store your question or the reply. We keep only a daily count of Mentor requests per account to enforce usage limits. Anthropic processes the request under its own commercial terms and privacy policy. Please do not include sensitive personal information about yourself or others in Mentor questions.

5. Subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Apple; we never receive your payment details. If you are signed in, the app sends Apple's signed record of your subscription to our server so Premium features such as Circles and the Mentor work on your account. We store the subscription's transaction ID, product, environment and expiry date.

6. Advertising (free plan)

The free plan shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. Premium has no ads. To show and measure ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects information from your device, which can include:

  • device identifiers, including Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking when iOS asks;
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • ad interactions (ads shown and tapped) and diagnostic data.

Google uses this information as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Stoia asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Stoia under Account › Ad privacy choices. You can also turn off tracking for all apps in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Stoia does not use any other analytics or advertising tools.

7. Service providers and where data is stored

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and server functions, in the United States.
  • Anthropic provides the AI model for the Mentor (United States).
  • Google provides ads on the free plan.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple, purchases, notifications and Apple Health.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it except with these providers to run the features described above, or when required by law.

8. How long we keep data

Account data, circle memberships and shared entries are kept until you delete them or your account. Subscription records are kept while your account exists. Mentor questions and replies are not kept. Usage counts for the Mentor are kept only as daily totals.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Use Stoia without an account, or sign out at any time from Account on the Today screen.
  • Delete shared circle entries in the app, or leave a circle.
  • Ask us to access, correct, export or delete your account data by emailing stoiaapp@gmail.com. We delete account data within 30 days of a verified request.
  • Change ad consent in the app, and tracking permission in iOS Settings.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or state privacy laws in the United States), you may have additional rights, including the right to object to processing and to complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Children

Stoia is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

Data sent between Stoia and our servers is encrypted in transit. Access to stored data is restricted so each person can reach only their own data and the circles they belong to. Your sign-in session is stored in your device's Keychain.

12. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above, and for significant changes we will let you know in the app.
